## 1. What is Content Engine Pro?

**Content Engine Pro** is an autonomous **WordPress Content Automation** plugin designed to automate the entire lifecycle of publishing high-quality, comprehensive articles, educational courses, and content series directly into your WordPress site.

It connects WordPress to free and custom AI providers (Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral, Cerebras) and orchestrates research, multi-part chunking (up to 6,000 words), SEO metadata, FAQ schema, internal linking, categorization, and minutely scheduled publishing without human intervention.

## 3. What Problem Does It Solve?

Simply asking a chatbot (like ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude) to write an article only solves 15% of the publishing workflow:
- You still have to prompt for an outline.
- Chatbots stop generating after 800-1200 words due to token limits.
- You have to manually format HTML headings, lists, and paragraphs.
- You have to log into WordPress, copy-paste content, and format code.
- You have to manually write meta descriptions and focus keywords.
- You have to manually find internal links and inject them.
- You have to configure categories, tags, slugs, and schema.
- You have to manually schedule dates and times.

**Content Engine Pro automates the remaining 85% of the workflow.**
